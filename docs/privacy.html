Privacy

Designed to keep your care records close.

CareCycle is a personal logging tool. It does not collect personal information, require an account, or make network requests.

Overview

CareCycle is designed with privacy as a core principle. The app helps you record infusion set changes and CGM sensor insertions on your iPhone. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice.

Data stored

The only data stored are the records you choose to enter, such as change dates, device type, and an optional sensor serial number. These records remain on your iPhone and are not linked to your identity by CareCycle.

Data sharing

CareCycle does not share data with third parties. The app makes no network requests and includes no analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting SDKs.

Camera

Camera access is requested only when you choose to scan a sensor serial number. Camera frames are processed on-device and are not saved or transmitted by CareCycle.

Widgets

The Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets read the same locally stored device status through Apple’s on-device App Group storage. Widget data does not leave your device through CareCycle.

Notifications

If you enable reminders, notifications are scheduled locally on your iPhone. Reminder preferences and content are not sent to a server.

Data deletion

You can delete all records at any time from Settings → Delete all records. Removing the app removes the data stored by the app from the device.

Children

CareCycle does not knowingly collect data from children. All records remain on-device and are not linked to an identifier.

Medical disclaimer

CareCycle is for personal logging and reminders only. It has not been reviewed or approved by the FDA, CE, or any equivalent regulatory authority as a medical device. Always follow the instructions provided by your device manufacturer and consult your healthcare provider or diabetes care team for medical decisions. Do not rely solely on this app to determine when to change medical supplies.
