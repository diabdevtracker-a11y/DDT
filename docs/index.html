A calmer kind of tracking

When the question is “when did we last change it?”, there should be a calm answer.

Diabetes care has enough alarms, charts, and decisions. CareCycle focuses on one small but recurring job: remembering device changes and seeing what is still in its window.

It is intentionally simple enough to use in the middle of a busy day, and thoughtful enough to make the next change easier to plan.

The mental load

Dates live in notes, messages, calendars, or memory — until someone needs the answer quickly.

The calmer routine

Private by design, not as an afterthought.

CareCycle stores the records you enter on your iPhone and does not make network requests. Camera frames used for serial-number scanning are processed on-device and are not saved or transmitted.
